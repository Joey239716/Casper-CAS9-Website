## Summary
Stopped at round 3 under D-001. `src/scene/cas9.js` loads without errors, all five params, six anchors and four views work, and it docks on the helix (`model=cas9,helix&view=docked`). The glass form is the real Cas9 surface from PDB 5F9R (no stylized fallback); the red thread is the sgRNA backbone from the same file, deliberately simplified (see Known issues). ## Interface delivered

### Source structure (for the references section)
- **PDB 5F9R.** Jiang F, Taylor DW, Chen JS, Kornfeld JE, Zhou K, Thompson AJ, Nogales E, Doudna JA. Structures of a CRISPR-Cas9 R-loop complex primed for DNA cleavage. *Science*. 2016;351(6275):867-871. doi:10.1126/science.aad8282. PMID 26841432. Crystal structure, 3.4 Å, catalytically active *S. pyogenes* Cas9 with sgRNA and double-stranded target DNA. Downloaded from https://files.rcsb.org/download/5F9R.pdb.
- **Glass surface:** chain B (Cas9), residues 3-1364, all heavy atoms (the file has no gaps in this range).
  - REC lobe = chain B residues 56-718. NUC lobe = chain B residues 3-55 and 719-1364. Boundary taken from UniProt Q99ZW2 ("Recognition lobe", 56-718), fetched from the UniProt REST API, not from memory.
- **Thread:** chain A (sgRNA, 116 nt). C4' of the 5' GTP (nt 1), then P atoms of nt 2-22 and nt 68-116. nt 23-67 are omitted (see Known issues).
- **Axis:** cylinder fit to the P atoms of chain A nt 2-20 and chain C (target DNA strand) nt 11-30, i.e. the 20-bp guide:target heteroduplex. Pairing was checked from the coordinates.
- **Active sites:** `hnh` = side-chain centroid of His840 (UniProt active site 840). `ruvc` = mean of the side-chain centroids of Asp10, Glu762, His983, Asp986 (Asp10 is UniProt active site 10; the other three are the UniProt Mn-binding residues 762 and 983 plus Asp986).
- Other candidates looked at: 4UN3 and 4OO8 both have several missing loops in the protein chain (4UN3: 385, 711-718, 766-775, 1013-1029, 1051-1058, 1242-1252; 4OO8: more) and are nuclease-inactive mutants; 5F9R is complete and catalytically active, so its HNH and RuvC positions are the ones at work.

### Frame and size
- 1 unit = 1 nm. DNA channel = local +Y through the origin; origin = centre of the heteroduplex (the 20-bp target).
- **PAM-proximal end of the target is at -Y; the free 5' tip of the spacer is at +Y.** If the helix puts its PAM on the +Y side, rotate the cas9 group 180 degrees about X or Z.
- REC lobe sits on -X, NUC lobe on +X. The lobes meet (hinge) on the -Z side and part on the +Z side, so +Z is the open face and the best camera side.
- Bounding box at rest: x -4.20..5.50, y -5.52..4.17, z -4.29..3.19 (about 9.7 x 9.7 x 7.5 nm). Not centred on the origin: the visual centre is about (0.6, -0.6, -0.4).
- Channel: measured on the built mesh, no glass vertex is closer than 2.24 nm to the Y axis in the closed pose (design floor 1.95 nm; `grip=1` brings the walls in by roughly 0.1 nm). The thread's channel coil is at 1.50 nm from the axis.
- `create(ctx)` is **async** (fetches `public/models/cas9.json` and `cas9.hi.bin` or `cas9.lo.bin`).

### Params (all 0..1, clamped; pure functions of value and time)
| Param | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `open` | 0 | Lobes swing apart about a hinge parallel to Y through (-1.01, y, -2.02): REC by 30 degrees, NUC by 20 degrees at 1, eased (smoothstep). 0 = closed. |
| `guide` | **1** | Thread drawn along its path from the 3' scaffold end on the outside of the NUC lobe (0) to the spacer's 5' tip at the top of the channel (1), with a tapered leading tip. 0 = no thread at all (mesh hidden). The channel coil occupies roughly the last 60% of the range. Set it to 0 before the `reader` scene. |
| `grip` | 0 | Each lobe closes a further 1.3 degrees and the idle breath is reduced by 70%. Subtle. |
| `cutPulse` | 0 | Red flare at both nuclease sites: bell `sin(pi*p)^1.6`, zero at 0 and 1, peak at 0.5. Opaque glowing cores (seen refracted through the glass) plus additive halos. |
| `presence` | 1 | 0 = `group.visible = false`. Rising: scale 0.86 -> 1.0, glass opacity 0 -> 1 (full by about 0.85), thread thickens from nothing (0.15 -> 0.9) and brightens. |

Idle motion from `time`: the lobes breathe open by up to 0.45 degrees at 0.8 rad/s. It only ever opens from the closed pose.

## Known issues
- **Over budget on frame time** (about 8 ms by the forced-sync measure above, budget 3 ms). The cost is the transmission pass plus the extra environment samples and the inner-wall pass. Cheapest levers: `BODY.wall = 0` (drops the inner-wall mesh's two draws; the mesh would also need removing to save them), or fewer dispersion samples.
- **Not five rounds, no `final-*.png` set**, and not every param was shot at 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1. Checked visually: `open` 0 and 1, `guide` 0.5 and 1, `cutPulse` 0.5, `presence` 0.6 and 1. `grip` and the breath were never looked at in a screenshot. `presence` between 0 and 0.3 and `open` at intermediate values are untested.
- **Docking at `helix.unzip=1` was not tested.** The thread's channel coil sits at 1.50 nm from the axis; the helix's open bubble may reach 1.7 nm, so the glass strands will probably cross the thread there.
- **The thread is a simplification, not the full sgRNA.** (1) The spacer is moved from its real radius (about 0.97 nm) out to 1.45 nm so the site's DNA fits inside it. (2) nt 23-67, the repeat:anti-repeat duplex and tetraloop, are left out: in the crystal they stack on the end of the heteroduplex exactly where the site's straight DNA runs (the real DNA bends away there). The thread bridges round the channel from nt 22 to nt 68. (3) The scaffold (nt 68-116) is low-pass filtered, so the stem loops are one soft curve. (4) The thread is drawn 3' to 5', which is a visual choice, not a claim about how the RNA loads.
- **The channel is carved, not real.** A 1.95 nm radius cylinder is removed along the heteroduplex axis; real Cas9 holds the duplex more tightly and the real DNA is bent. The surface is also inflated (low iso level) to keep the REC lobe in one piece after carving. The `open` hinge is a visual device placed where the lobes meet, not the real conformational change.
- Thread kinks: a small V where the coil meets the scaffold and a zigzag at the 3' end.
- Glass is brighter and busier than intended; `BODY` constants were not tuned.
- `quality=low`: the shared low glass is opaque, so the thread is hidden wherever a lobe is in front of it, and the 0.21 nm mesh shows a slightly ragged edge along the seam of the REC lobe.
- Clear glass does not show other clear glass, so the far lobe and the helix's backbones disappear behind the near lobe (expected, CONTRACT section 5); the helix's frosted rungs and the thread do show through.
- With `presence < 1` the glass switches to a second, transparent material; the first frame that uses it compiles a shader (possible one-frame hitch when Cas9 first fades in).
